## Deadlines (§ 28)

| Case | Deadline | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic supply | 15 days from the day the tax obligation arose | § 28 odst. 8 |
| Service to a business in another EU state | 15 days from the end of the month of supply | § 28 odst. 9 písm. b) |
| Service to a business outside the EU | 15 days from the end of the month of supply | § 28 odst. 9 písm. c) |
| Advance received before an EU-business service | 15 days from the end of the month of receipt | § 28 odst. 9 písm. d), § 24a odst. 3 |

## Other rules used by the core

- Exchange rate: the rate valid for the converting person on the day the obligation arises, ČNB or ECB (§ 4 odst. 8).
- Tax arithmetic: base × rate, or top-down with coefficient 1.21 / 1.12 (§ 37).
- Retention: 10 years from the end of the tax period of the supply (§ 35 odst. 2); electronic storage must keep integrity and authenticity data (§ 35a odst. 3).
- Souhrnné hlášení: EU-business services under § 9 odst. 1, including sufficiently known advances, values in CZK (§ 102 odst. 1 písm. d), odst. 4).
