| --- | --- | --- |

| Goods to a business in another EU state, exempt under § 64 | 15 days from the end of the month of supply | § 28 odst. 9 písm. a) |

## Other rules used by the core

- Exchange rate: the rate valid for the converting person on the day the obligation arises, ČNB or ECB (§ 4 odst. 8).
- Tax arithmetic: base × rate, or top-down with coefficient 1.21 / 1.12 (§ 37).
- Retention: 10 years from the end of the tax period of the supply (§ 35 odst. 2); electronic storage must keep integrity and authenticity data (§ 35a odst. 3).
- Souhrnné hlášení: goods supplied to a business registered for VAT in another member state (§ 102 odst. 1 písm. a)); EU-business services under § 9 odst. 1, including sufficiently known advances, values in CZK (§ 102 odst. 1 písm. d), odst. 4).
- Goods to a business in another member state (§ 64 odst. 1): exempt with the right to deduct when the customer gave its VAT ID (písm. a)), the goods leave the Czech Republic for another member state (písm. b)) and the supply is in the souhrnné hlášení (písm. c)). The document cites the exemption (§ 29 odst. 2 písm. a)) and has no rate or VAT (§ 29 odst. 3 písm. e) bod 1). The supply is declared on the 15th day of the month after it, or on the earlier issue date (§ 22 odst. 1), and a document of such goods alone converts its CZK values at the rate valid on that day (§ 4 odst. 8). A payment received before it needs no payment tax document: § 20a odst. 2 covers taxable supplies only (§ 2 odst. 2), so § 28 odst. 1 písm. d) does not apply (ACCT).
